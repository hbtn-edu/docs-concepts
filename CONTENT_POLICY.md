# Content and source policy

## Scope of the repository license

Unless a document or asset states otherwise, original content created for this repository is available under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International license described in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

The repository license applies only to material that Holberton has the right to license. It does not replace or override the rights, licenses, or attribution requirements that apply to third-party material.

## Sources

Documents should identify their principal sources in a `Sources` or `References` section. A source being publicly accessible on the internet does not by itself make that source public domain or grant permission to reproduce it.

Repository content should:

- explain and reorganize concepts using original wording;
- credit the principal sources used to prepare each document;
- preserve any attribution or license notice required by a source;
- clearly identify direct quotations and keep them limited to what is necessary;
- avoid reproducing third-party images, diagrams, tables, code, or substantial passages unless their terms permit it; and
- identify material governed by a license different from the repository's CC BY-NC 4.0 license.

## Recommended source entry

```text
- Author or organization. "Title." URL. Accessed YYYY-MM-DD.
  License: license name or "No license identified."
  Use in this document: reference, quotation, adapted diagram, or other concise description.
```

## Attribution by downstream users

When sharing or adapting eligible repository content, users should identify Holberton and this repository, provide a link to the original material and the CC BY-NC 4.0 license, and indicate whether changes were made.

Use of third-party material remains subject to its original terms.
